How ChurnSight reads your data

  • One requirement: a churn column (churn, Churn, Exited…) with 0/1 or Yes/No.
  • Everything else is detected: customer ID, dates, numeric and categorical features. Telco, bank and SaaS exports all work as-is.
  • Honest evaluation: with a snapshot date the holdout is the latest 20% in time, like production. Otherwise a stratified split.
  • Revenue-aware: include a column such as monthly_charges or mrr to rank accounts by revenue at risk.
  • Better with behaviour: rolling usage, ticket and payment signals beat static profile fields by a wide margin.
